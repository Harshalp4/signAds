# Future photography and footage

Capture original landscape files, preferably 4000 px or wider, with a second portrait framing for mobile. Keep RAW/original JPEG masters and record date, location, photographer and permission.

1. Finished storefront, straight-on daytime wide shot. Repeat the same framing at dusk/night with the sign illuminated.
2. Reception branding: wide space plus a close view of the letters. Photograph the face, depth, mounting and light spill without altering branding.
3. Metal, PU colour and ACP samples: macro texture, edge and finish shots with a neutral colour reference.
4. Building facade: safe ground-level wide view and a closer sign view. Avoid identifiable bystanders where possible.
5. Print products: real approved visiting cards, flyers, brochures and banners. Capture paper edges, folded states and a readable overall layout.
6. Printing and fabrication: short stable clips of approved operations, materials and finishing. Confirm permission from workers shown.
7. Installation: record only from an approved safe position. Do not stage risky activity for the camera.
8. Transit/outdoor: complete vehicle or placement in context, clean side view and readable detail. Record placement ownership and campaign permissions.

For future homepage footage: 6–10 seconds per shot, stable 16:9 framing, no music required, locked exposure and short gentle camera movement. Avoid rapid cuts or flashing light. Produce 1280 × 720 silent MP4/WebM plus a matching still. Until real footage is supplied, the homepage keeps a real project still and category heroes use explicitly illustrative animated material studies.
